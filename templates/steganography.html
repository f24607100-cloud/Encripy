analytics How does it work?

This tool uses Least Significant Bit (LSB) steganography. It converts your text into binary (0s and 1s) and hides these bits in the smallest color value of each pixel in the image. The change is so extremely subtle that the human eye cannot detect any difference in the image colors, even under magnification.
